# USDC Bridge (qUSDC)

> **Status: planned, not yet enabled.**

qUSDC — a wrapped, 1:1 representation of USDC on RougeChain — is planned but **not yet
enabled** on RougeChain. USDC deposits are not claimable and USDC withdrawals are not
served by the relayer.

The bridge assets available today are **qETH** (see [ETH Bridge](eth-bridge.md)) and
**XRGE** (see [XRGE Bridge](xrge-bridge.md)). This page will be filled in with deposit
and withdrawal steps once qUSDC bridging is turned on.
